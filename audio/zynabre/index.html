05.Zynabre

ZynAddSubFX ported to Rust. Loads your .xiz and .xmz patches.

Zynabre is a port of the ZynAddSubFX engine (3.0.x), module by module from the C++ sources to Rust. It loads the original .xiz and .xmz patch files and plays them the way Zyn does, which was verified sample for sample against the original.

It includes ADDsynth, SUBsynth and PADsynth, 16 parts × 16 kit items and the full effect rack, inside a new editor.

“Twenty years of sounds were waiting behind it.”

  • _ ZynAddSubFX, 2002
  • _ Additive · subtractive · PAD synth
  • _ 16 parts × 16 kit items
  • _ 1,517 instruments in 42 banks
