Tonsetzer

MUSIKKOMPONIST DAW

A DAW that presents itself like a sheet of music paper.

Open source on GitHub ↗ macOS · Apple Silicon · MIT
01

THE PAPER

Instead of one endless horizontal strip, time wraps onto the next system every few bars, as on music paper. Audio clips and live notation share the same systems. A Tonsetzer is a composer, cousin to the Schriftsetzer, the typesetter: paper, ink, five primaries. Red appears only when something is recording.

02

HOW A PIECE STARTS

A new project opens with three tracks ready: a glockenspiel on a grand staff, a drum track on Schlag, the built-in FM kit, and an audio track. You can sketch before choosing a single plug-in; AUv3 instruments and effects load in Devices when you want them.

PLAY OR WRITE

Arm a track, press REC: one bar of count-in, then notation appears as you play. Or pick the note in the top bar and write on the staff; the pointer becomes the note itself.

BRING SOUND IN

Drop a recording onto the window. A file that carries its tempo follows the song. Record from your interface, or resample another track.

SHAPE AND HAND OVER

Loop a passage, grow it into Sätze, balance the mix. Export the mix, stems, single Sätze or MIDI. The project itself stays readable text.

03

NOTATION & MIDI

What you play is never rewritten. The staff is a reading of it: spelled by key, beamed and tied at the resolution you choose, with grand staff and changes of clef, key, meter and tempo anywhere. Writing by hand never changes what is already there: a value that does not fit the bar turns red. Drums, or anything a staff describes poorly, can be viewed as MIDI.

04

SÄTZE

A Satz is a movement: a score of its own on the same instruments, with its own tempo and meter. Loop a passage and press + to give it a seal and a colour. Insert it anywhere as a hard link: edit the refrain once, and every occurrence follows.

05

MIX & AUTOMATION

Each strip holds its input, its plug-in chain and its sends; send tracks carry shared effects, and latency is compensated on every path. Automation lines move volume, pan, a send, or any parameter a plug-in exposes.
